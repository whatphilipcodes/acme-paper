### `Overall Review`

The primary novelty of this paper is its contribution to accessibility, decreasing the barrier to entry for Interactive Machine Learning within the TouchDesigner ecosystem. The project introduces a high-level, modular toolkit that allows users to engage with Machine Learning (ML) concepts through a node-based workflow. As a tool for media art, the project is a suitable fit for Creative Technologies, as it operates at the intersection of performative art and interactive technology.

A weakness of the paper is the absence of an in-code implementation provided alongside the submission, which is particularly relevant given the lack of user studies. Without access to the toolkit, a full assessment of its usability is not possible. Furthermore, providing concrete performance measurements could strengthen the claims made in terms of the trade-off between performance and ease of use. Benchmarking the CPU-based models against GPU-based alternatives would help quantify this concretely.

Despite these omissions, the paper has several strengths: It is exceptionally well-positioned within the context of related projects, as detailed in Section 2, addressing a research gap while setting itself apart from other existing tools. The paper credibly presents a working and well-considered prototype, with an extensive and clearly structured breakdown of the implemented modules in Section 3. Together with the usage examples (Section 4) this makes for a well-rounded and convincing presentation of a potentially valuable creative tool.


### `Overall Rating: 4/5`

- functionality / usability unclear
- mention: consideration for best paper award -> practical potentially useful contribution

---
The paper presents a well-structured and credible contribution. The lack of a public code release or user study make its real-world functionality and usability unclear, therefore holding it back from an 'excellent' rating.

OSC (Open Sound Control)). The pattern throughout the whole paper should be as follows: Full Term (FT) followed by the usage of FT for the remaining part of the paper.
- overall inconsistent citation formatting (eg. section 2, paragraph 2, line 5: extra brackets around [citation] and inconsistent spacing repeated multiple times in the following sections)
- usage of "in-graph" without explanation might be confusing to audience with no prior TouchDesigner experience
